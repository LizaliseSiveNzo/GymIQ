# Food image prompts (Google Flow)

save it with the filename shown (matching `web/public/foods/` naming in `README.md`).

**Shared style** (already baked into every prompt): photorealistic food photography, subject centered on a
matte dark charcoal slate surface, soft diffused studio lighting, 45-degree overhead angle, square 1:1,
subject fills most of the frame, appetizing and clean, no text, no labels, no hands, no cutlery, no
packaging, no logos. If the generator adds text or a watermark, regenerate.
