## Base prompt — paste first, for every image

Create an original instructional exercise illustration for GymIQ. Use a clean premium 3D clay-render style, a matte charcoal background (#10140D), soft studio lighting and a faint ground shadow. Use the same fictional adult training mannequin throughout: natural athletic proportions, warm light-grey surface, simplified facial features, fitted charcoal sportswear, white trainers and small electric-lime (#C6FF3A) accents. Equipment is neutral grey with clear mechanically plausible parts. Prioritize readable anatomy and form over drama. No real-person likeness or copied branding. Keep athlete, clothing, equipment, lighting and scale consistent across the panels. Keep the camera fixed for movement phases; only use different views when this exercise explicitly requests two angles of a static hold. Use separate clearly spaced panels with the same ground line; never superimpose multiple limbs on one body. Frame the complete athlete and all necessary equipment, including hands, feet, grips, cable anchors and contact points. Leave a small safe margin. Produce a sharp landscape 16:9 still image suitable for enlarging on a phone. Do not render exercise names, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks, arrows or other text; these will be added in the app.

AVOID: extra or missing limbs or fingers; fused hands and handles; disconnected cables; floating or distorted equipment; impossible joint bends; inconsistent left/right limbs; obstructed hands or feet; oversized loads; extreme ranges of motion; exaggerated bodybuilding proportions; motion blur; busy gym backgrounds; decorative effects that conceal form. Render intentional spinal movement only where this specific exercise requires it. Do not invent a movement variant.

## Exercise blocks — append one block to the base prompt

### 1. Bodyweight Squat
EXERCISE: Bodyweight Squat.
CAMERA: side view.
SEQUENCE AND SETUP: Two panels. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out, arms extended forward. Start standing tall. End in a deep squat with thighs parallel to the floor and a straight back.
CRITICAL FORM AND EQUIPMENT DETAILS: Keep the chest lifted and the knees tracking over the toes; show full foot contact and forward-extended arms.
AVOID: knees collapsing inward, heels lifting, excessive forward lean, exaggerated depth below parallel.

### 2. Jump Squat
EXERCISE: Jump Squat.
CAMERA: side view.
SEQUENCE AND SETUP: Two panels. Panel one: a partial squat with the arms swinging back. Panel two: fully airborne with both feet off the ground, knees tucked, arms swinging up.
CRITICAL FORM AND EQUIPMENT DETAILS: Both feet clearly clear of the ground; show an athletic, controlled mid-air posture.
AVOID: rigid straight legs mid-air, exaggerated poses, motion blur.

### 3. Pistol Squat
EXERCISE: Pistol Squat.
CAMERA: side view.
SEQUENCE AND SETUP: Two panels. Stand on one leg with the free leg extended forward. Start tall. End in a deep single-leg squat with the free leg held straight and parallel to the floor, arms reaching forward.
CRITICAL FORM AND EQUIPMENT DETAILS: Flat working foot, heel down, free leg fully extended, arms counterbalancing.
AVOID: heel lift, free leg bending, torso collapsing.

### 4. Bodyweight Lunge
EXERCISE: Bodyweight Lunge.
CAMERA: side view.
SEQUENCE AND SETUP: Two panels. Start standing tall with hands on hips. End in a forward lunge with the front knee at 90 degrees and the rear knee near the floor.
CRITICAL FORM AND EQUIPMENT DETAILS: Upright torso, front shin vertical, rear knee just above the ground.
AVOID: front knee driving far past the toes, torso leaning, rear knee slamming.

### 5. Bodyweight Split Squat

CRITICAL FORM AND EQUIPMENT DETAILS: Vertical front shin, upright torso, rear heel lifted naturally.
